Figure 2. Separate Gamma log-link models include linear and squared age, June–August rainfall and mean temperature for each hybrid, using 26 measurement years. Rainfall is contrasted from 122.44 to 222.44 mm at 15.81 °C; temperature is contrasted from 15.31 to 16.31 °C at 172.44 mm rainfall. Differences are averaged equally over the 26 observed ages and expressed in Mg C ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹. Points show absolute mean PAIC differences; whiskers show ±1 model-based SE calculated by the delta method, not 95% confidence intervals. The dashed line marks zero. Weather describes the preceding growth year, including annualised multi-year intervals.
