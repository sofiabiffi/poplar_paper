Figure 2 Annualised SOC accumulation estimated as each complete agroforestry profile's ESM stock minus the mean measured fixed-depth arable stock, divided by the agreed duration (19 years for former Continuous cropping alleys; 32 years for Continuous fallow alleys and tree rows). Points show group means and error bars show SD among profile-level annualised estimates. The shared arable baseline is held fixed; SD is not a confidence interval. Arable zero is the defined reference. These are retrospective stock contrasts, not directly measured annual fluxes.
