Figure 1 SOC stocks for arable reference, alley and tree-row profiles. Arable stocks remain measured fixed-depth 0-15 cm and 15-30 cm values. Alley and tree-row stocks are ESM corrected to the corresponding arable mean reference masses. Boxes show median and IQR; whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 times the IQR. Points are cores. Dunn letters use BH adjustment across three pairs within each panel, p < 0.05. Shared letters indicate no detected difference.
