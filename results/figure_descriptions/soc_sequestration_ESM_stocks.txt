Figure 1 SOC stocks in the arable reference, alley fallow and tree rows. Arable stocks are measured at fixed depth (0-30 cm); agroforestry stocks are ESM corrected to the arable 0-30 cm reference mass. Points show complete-profile means and error bars show SD among profiles, not confidence intervals. The dashed line marks the measured fixed-depth arable mean. Groups comprise 47 arable profiles, eight profiles in each selected alley group and 21 tree-row profiles.
