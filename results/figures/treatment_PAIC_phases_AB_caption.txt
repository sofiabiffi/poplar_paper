Figure 1. Model-adjusted periodic annual carbon increment (PAIC) during active cropping (A) and after cropping ceased (B), averaged equally across four poplar hybrids. Treatment labels in B refer to the former cropping treatments. Points show estimated marginal means; error bars show pointwise 95% confidence intervals. An asterisk marks continuous cropping versus continuous fallow and a plus marks continuous cropping versus alternate cropping, using Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 across all three treatment pairs and all observed intervals within each phase (21 tests in A; 54 in B). Markers denote differences in either direction; absence of a marker does not establish equality. Year denotes the measurement year and PAIC is annualised over the preceding measurement interval. The 2001 value averages growth years 1998-2000. The 2002-2005 boundary interval is excluded from both primary panels. Lines connect observed visits without imputing missing annual values.
